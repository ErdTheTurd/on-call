Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026 · Last updated: August 21, 2026

MD Shift (“MD Shift,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides shift trading and on-call coverage tools for doctors and hospitals through our website at mdshift.net and our iOS app (together, the “Service”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or otherwise using the Service, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Who this applies to

  • Real accounts — doctors, hospital staff, and admins who sign up or sign in. Account and scheduling data sync to our servers.
  • Demo / explore mode — walkthroughs that use mock sample data on your device only. Demo mode does not upload that sample data to our cloud.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use MD Shift, we may collect:

Account and contact information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Account credentials (passwords are handled by our auth provider; we do not store plaintext passwords)
  • Sign-in provider identifiers if you use Google or Sign in with Apple

Professional and profile information

  • Role (doctor or hospital)
  • Specialty and credential type (for example MD / DO)
  • NPI and license number / state (for verification)
  • Hospital or organization name and related profile fields
  • Credential documents you choose to upload (for example license, DEA, malpractice COI, board certification)

Scheduling and product content

  • Shifts, coverage requests, approvals, assignments, trades, and related status history
  • Rates, rate floors, and related pay / scheduling settings you configure
  • Roster and preference settings used to run coverage

Technical information

  • Account / user IDs needed to operate the Service
  • Basic device and browser information typical of secure web and app access (for example IP address in server logs)

We do not collect, for App Privacy purposes:

  • Precise or coarse location
  • Phone contacts / address book
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or advertising tracking profiles
  • HealthKit or clinical health-record data
  • Payment card numbers in the app (paid features, if enabled later, would use a payment provider)

Optional device features such as Face ID / Touch ID, when used to unlock the app, stay on your device and are not collected by MD Shift.

3. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, and secure the Service (accounts, scheduling, trades, approvals)
  • Verify clinician or hospital identity where required for coverage workflows
  • Send transactional email such as sign-up verification codes
  • Respond to support requests you send us
  • Improve reliability and fix issues
  • Comply with law and protect against abuse or fraud

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for cross-app advertising tracking.

4. How we share information

We share information only as needed to run the Service:

  • Within your coverage network — hospitals and doctors who interact on a shift, request, approval, or trade may see the professional details required for that workflow (for example name, specialty, relevant schedule data).
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf, including:
    • Supabase — authentication and database hosting
    • Resend — transactional email delivery (for example one-time codes)
    • Google / Apple — only if you choose those sign-in options
  • Legal / safety — if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security

5. Data retention

We retain account and scheduling information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account as described below.

6. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards appropriate to a cloud-hosted application, including encrypted transport (HTTPS), access controls, and authentication features such as email verification codes and optional authenticator-based multi-factor authentication. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please use a strong unique password and protect your devices.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access or update profile information in the app or website
  • Enable or disable optional MFA where available
  • Request account deletion or a copy of your data by contacting us
  • Opt out of demo mode at any time (demo data remains local)

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws (for example access, correction, deletion, or portability). To exercise those rights, contact us using the email below.

8. Children’s privacy

MD Shift is designed for licensed clinicians and healthcare organizations. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required by local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International users

We may process information in the United States and other countries where our providers operate. If you use the Service from another country, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version at this URL and revise the “Last updated” date. Continued use of the Service after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
