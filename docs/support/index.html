Common questions

I never got my verification code

Check spam for a message from MD Shift. Codes expire after a few minutes — request a new one from the app or sign-in page. Still nothing? Email us with the address you used.

I can’t get into my account

Try the same method you signed up with (email, Google, or Apple). If you turned on authenticator MFA, you’ll need the 6-digit code from that app. Locked out of MFA? Email us from the address on the account.

A shift, trade, or approval looks wrong

Tell us the date, hospital (if you know it), and what you expected to see. We’ll check the assignment on our side and reply with what we find.

Demo / sample data

Explore mode uses made-up sample shifts so you can click around without a live roster. It isn’t real coverage. Sign in with a real account when you’re ready to schedule for real.

Privacy & data

How we handle account and scheduling data is in the Privacy Policy. For a deletion request or privacy question, email the same support address and say it’s privacy-related.
